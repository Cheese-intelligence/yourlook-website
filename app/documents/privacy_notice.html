YourLook ("YourLook", "we", "our" or "us") is operated by Keystone Labs Ltd. For the purposes of UK data protection law, Keystone Labs Ltd is the controller of the Personal Data described in this Privacy Notice.

1.2 Our commitment to privacy

We are committed to processing Personal Data fairly, lawfully and transparently, and to protecting your privacy in accordance with applicable data protection law.

1.3 Scope of this Privacy Notice

This Privacy Notice explains what Personal Data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, how we protect it, and the rights you have when you use the Product or Service. Although Keystone Labs Ltd is established in the United Kingdom, the Product may be accessed by Users located in other jurisdictions. Where applicable data protection laws require additional rights or protections, we will comply with those mandatory legal requirements in addition to the protections described in this Privacy Notice.

1.4 Definitions

Capitalised terms used in this Privacy Notice have the meanings given in the Terms and Conditions, unless otherwise stated. "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.

We may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

When you use the Product or Service, we may automatically collect technical and usage information, including:

If you use the Product as a Professional, we may collect information relating to your salon, business, or professional profile, including location, opening hours, capacity, services, pricing, qualifications, availability, and other information you choose to provide.

We obtain Personal Data directly from you when you create an account, make a Booking, upload Content, communicate with us or otherwise use the Product.

2.6 Children's Data

The Product is intended for use by individuals aged 18 or over. Where a User is under the age of 18, Personal Data relating to that User will be processed only with the involvement of a parent or legal guardian where required by applicable law.

3. Biometric Data

3.1 What Biometric Data We Collect

Certain optional features of the Product may require the processing of Biometric Data, including facial images and facial embeddings generated from those images. Biometric-enabled features are optional. Where the User is under the age of 18, any consent required for biometric processing must be provided or authorized by a parent or legal guardian where required by applicable law.

3.2 Why We Collect It

Biometric Data is processed solely for the purposes of providing the relevant optional feature, such as hairstyle visualisation, facial analysis or secure authentication where available.

3.3 Health information

You may voluntarily provide health-related information, including allergies, skin or scalp conditions, medical treatments, or other information relevant to the services requested. Such information is processed only where necessary to provide the requested services and, where required by applicable law, based on your explicit consent. Access to such information is limited to authorized personnel and relevant Professionals involved in providing the requested services.

Where health-related information constitutes special category data, we rely on your explicit consent under Article 9 of the UK GDPR unless another lawful condition applies

3.4 Explicit Consent

We will collect and process Biometric Data only where you have provided separate, explicit, informed and freely given consent for the specific feature concerned. The use of biometric features is optional and is not required to access the core functionality of the Product.

Where Biometric Data is processed for uniquely identifying or authenticating you, such as secure facial authentication, Keystone Labs Ltd processes that data as special category personal data under Article 9 of the UK GDPR and relies on your explicit consent as the applicable lawful condition for processing.

Some optional image-analysis features, such as hairstyle visualization, may analyze facial characteristics without being used to uniquely identify you. Where such processing nevertheless constitutes Biometric Data under applicable law, we will obtain your explicit consent before processing.

3.5 Information Provided Before Processing

Before biometric processing takes place, we will explain the categories of Biometric Data collected, the specific purpose of the processing, whether processing takes place on your device, on our servers or through an authorised service provider, whether any third-party processor is involved, the general retention period applicable to the Biometric Data and how you may withdraw your consent.

3.6 Storage and Third-Party Processing

Biometric processing may take place on your device, on Keystone Labs Ltd servers, or through carefully selected third-party service providers acting on our behalf, depending on the functionality used. We take reasonable steps to ensure that all third-party providers processing biometric data are contractually required to protect such data in accordance with applicable data protection law and to process it solely on our documented instructions

Where biometric data is processed by third-party service providers, such providers act solely as data processors on behalf of Keystone Labs Ltd. These providers may include cloud hosting providers and biometric technology providers. We require all such providers to implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures and to process biometric data only for the purposes authorized by Keystone Labs Ltd.

3.7 Security of Biometric Data

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Biometric Data, given its sensitive nature. These measures may include, where appropriate, encryption in transit and encryption at rest. We monitor system-level security events through appropriate logging and monitoring processes.

3.8 Withdrawal of Consent

You may withdraw your consent to biometric processing at any time through your account settings or by contacting us. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, but it will disable the relevant biometric feature.

3.9 Deletion of Biometric Data

Unless we are required by law to retain it, your Biometric Data will be deleted or permanently de-identified without undue delay following withdrawal of consent or when the relevant biometric feature is disabled, in accordance with our retention and deletion practices.

For privacy-related enquiries, please contact us at: support@yourlook.app

10. Security

10.1 Security Measures

Depending on the nature, scope, context, and purposes of the processing, and the risks to individuals' rights and freedoms, we implement appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, or access.

Technical measures

  • Secure cloud infrastructure and hosting solutions designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Personal Data.
  • Monitoring and logging of system-level security and technical events to support the detection, investigation, and response to security incidents.
  • encryption of Personal Data during storage ("at rest") and transmission ("in transit"), where appropriate, using appropriate cryptographic safeguards; and
  • role-based access controls, authentication mechanisms, and database-level security measures designed to restrict access to Personal Data to authorized personnel with a legitimate business need.

Organizational measures

  • restricting access to Personal Data on a need-to-know basis.
  • confidentiality obligations for personnel with access to Personal Data.
  • internal policies and procedures governing the handling of Personal Data; and
  • periodic review of our security practices.

The security measures we implement may evolve to reflect changes in technology, operational requirements, and legal obligations.

10.2 Security Limitations

While we take reasonable steps to protect Personal Data, no method of electronic transmission, storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, we cannot guarantee that Personal Data will never be accessed, disclosed, altered or destroyed by unauthorized third parties.

10.3 Personal Data Breaches

If a Personal Data breach occurs, we will investigate the incident and comply with any applicable legal obligations, including notifying the Information Commissioner's Office and affected individuals where required by law.
